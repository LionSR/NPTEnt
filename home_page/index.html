NPT Bound Entanglement (NPTEnt)

NPTEnt is a Lean 4 investigation of Islam Faisal’s preprint Bound Entanglement with Negative Partial Transpose Exists (arXiv:2611.34921v1, 2026). The central question is whether there exists a bipartite quantum state with negative partial transpose (NPT) that cannot be distilled into maximally entangled pairs by local operations and classical communication.

The preprint proposes a single-copy operator certificate aimed at bounding the all-copy two-qubit Bell overlap under separable operations for the two-qudit Werner state

ρ = 23I − 3F1448 on ℂ8 ⊗ ℂ8,

where F denotes the swap operator. Reusable quantum-information foundations for this work are being developed upstream in QICLean, while paper-specific states and finite witness data remain in NPTEnt.

Full undistillability / bound-entanglement conclusion (incl. Dür–Cirac–Lewenstein–Bruß all-N Schmidt-rank-≤2 criterion) Not yet proved

The single-copy certificate, canonical all-copy Bell-overlap cap, and zero finite-round, finite-outcome LOCC distillation rate for ρ are Lean 4 verified, while the full undistillability conclusion (including the all-copy Schmidt-rank-≤2 partial-transpose criterion) remains unproved here; declaration counts are not a measure of completion. Consult the blueprint and paper-gap notes for current mathematical coverage and open steps.
