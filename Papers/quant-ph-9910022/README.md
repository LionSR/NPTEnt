# Dür–Cirac–Lewenstein–Bruß: distillability and partial transposition

W. Dür, J. I. Cirac, M. Lewenstein and D. Bruß, *Distillability and partial
transposition in bipartite systems*, Physical Review A **61**, 062313 (2000).

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.61.062313
- arXiv version consulted: https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9910022v1
- Full text: https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/9910022v1
- First submitted 6 October 1999; journal publication 17 May 2000.
- Bibliographic metadata checked against arXiv and Crossref.

Section II.2, equations (3)–(4), formulates N-copy distillability through a
Schmidt-rank-at-most-two vector with negative expectation against the
Alice-side partial transpose of the N-copy state. The Schmidt rank is across
all Alice copies versus all Bob copies. Undistillability requires nonnegative
expectation for every such vector and every positive copy number. The paper
relates this to two-dimensional local projections and cites Horodecki and
Horodecki (1999) for the operational reduction.

This finite-copy criterion is kept distinct from the asymptotic rate quantity
in the formalization. In particular, a two-qubit filtered output with Bell
fraction above one half must be amplified before it contradicts a uniform
cap strictly between one half and one. No operational or rate equivalence is
accepted merely by citation.

For the state considered in NPTEnt, the authors’ Werner convention
`rho_alpha = (S_d + alpha A_d) / Tr(S_d + alpha A_d)`, with
`S_d = (I+F)/2` and `A_d = (I-F)/2`, gives `d=8`, `alpha=13/10`
and their partial-transpose parameter `beta=1/23`. This is a parameter
translation, not an invocation of their undistillability conjecture.
